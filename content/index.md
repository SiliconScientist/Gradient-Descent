Welcome to the shared dossier from our completed **Gradient Descent** campaign for **Mothership**, a science-fiction horror tabletop role-playing game.

Choose a note below to start exploring, or use **Search** to find a name or clue. Follow links within a note to discover connections; on desktop, each note also has an interactive graph.

Notes separate **Facts** from **Unverified** reports, preserving what we learned and what remained unresolved.
